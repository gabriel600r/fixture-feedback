## Football doesn't stop. Neither does the app.

Fixture Fútbol is the app for following the leagues and cups you care about, from your phone and without ads. You pick your competitions and the app downloads only those.

### ⚽ 25 leagues and cups

**Argentina** — Liga Profesional, Primera Nacional, Copa Argentina, Supercopa Argentina
**South America** — Copa Libertadores, Copa Sudamericana
**Brazil** — Brasileirão Série A, Copa do Brasil
**Europe** — Champions League and Europa League, with their qualifying rounds · Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1
**North America** — MLS, Liga MX, Leagues Cup, Concacaf Champions
**Rest of South America** — Peru, Colombia, Chile, Ecuador, Paraguay, Bolivia

Some are out of season: they stay loaded and show up on their own once they're playing again.

### 📊 Live, no refreshing

Score and minute update on their own. While your team is playing, the match stays **pinned to your notification bar** with the latest goal on top: you check it without opening the app. Tap the card and you land straight in the match.

### 🔔 Alerts that actually arrive

Goal, kick-off and full time for the matches you choose. The goal alert says who scored and who assisted. And if VAR rules it out, we tell you that goal is gone.

You pick which ones you want: you can ask for goals only and nothing else. Star your favourite teams, or ring the bell on a single match.

### 📋 The match at a glance

Goals, cards and substitutions in one list, latest on top, and each half closes by telling you the score at that point. Below the match: the referee, the stadium, the date and the weather. If it goes to penalties, the shootout looks like it does on TV: who took it, who scored and who missed. And you share it to the group in two taps.

### 👕 Line-ups on the pitch

The eleven laid out on the field and the substitutes on the bench. Tap a player for their age, height, foot and nationality.

### 📈 League tables

Every league's table, up to date, with the tournament currently being played at the top.

### 🏆 Cup brackets

First leg, second leg, aggregate score and who goes through. No maths required.

### 📅 Fixtures and season history

The calendar runs from four days back to four days ahead. And the History tab has each league's full season, with a search by team.

### 📺 Where to watch each match

Pick your country in Settings and every match shows you which channel is airing it. It already works in 18 countries.

### 🎯 Only what's yours

Tick the competitions that matter to you and the rest isn't downloaded: less data, less battery and a screen without noise. No account, no sign-up: you open it and it works. And your favourite teams' matches always show up, whether you follow that league or not.

### 🗂️ The national-team tournament, kept

The 104 matches played in June and July are still inside the app: results, groups, brackets and the champion. Free, to go back to whenever you want.

### 🌐 Languages

The app is in Spanish, English and Portuguese, chosen in Settings. Italian and French are on the way.

## How the app keeps going

Fixture started as a family thing: a World Cup fixture list so we could follow the matches between us. It got out of hand, and when the World Cup ended I decided to carry on with the leagues.

**There are no ads here, and there never will be.** It isn't a marketing stance: they ruin the experience. You open the app for a score and you have to dodge a banner.

The downside is that without ads the app doesn't pay for itself: the servers and the live data cost money every month. The only way this keeps going is together, with **a coffee a month** from inside the app. On its own it's little; between many, it's enough.

Live scores, alerts for your teams, tables, brackets and the season history are **free and will stay free**. The coffee adds the line-ups drawn on the pitch, the player of the match, each player's stat sheet, the fine-grained statistics and alerts for every match. And if you want, your name appears on the wall inside the app.

Thanks to everyone who has already bought one. ⚽

## FAQ

**I open the app and it's empty / I still see the World Cup.**
You're on an old version. Update from Google Play and the leagues, live matches and notifications come back.

**Notifications don't reach me.**
Check that the app has notification permission and that the team is starred with the bell. On some phones you have to take the app out of battery saving so it can alert you with the screen off.

**I can't see which channel is showing the match.**
Pick your country in Settings. Channels cover 18 countries for now; if yours isn't there, tell me in a ticket.

**A match or a league is missing.**
Tell me which one in a [ticket](../../issues/new/choose). The 25 above are the ones available today; more get added based on what people ask for.

**Is the data official?**
It comes from a sports data provider. Sometimes it takes a while to confirm a goal or the scorer's name; when the provider corrects it, the app corrects itself.

## Privacy

The app **asks for no account and no sign-up**. What you choose (favourite teams, leagues, alerts) is stored on your phone, not on a server. The coffee purchase is handled by Google Play; we neither see nor store your card details.
